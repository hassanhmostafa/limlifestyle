# Events Lifestyle Report Catalog: Reviewed Provenance

**Scope.** This document records the limited, reviewed evidence used for the deterministic, participant-facing **strengths** and **next steps** panels in Events. It does not claim that any full book or its 511 pages were ingested, and it contains no scanned pages or copyrighted extracts.

## Design safeguards

- The report only summarizes completed questionnaire answers; partial or missing answers remain explicitly unavailable.
- The score formula and its existing `150` weekly-minute activity criterion are unchanged.
- Strengths are relative to the participant's completed pillar scores. They do not declare an absence of symptoms, risk, or clinical need.
- Next steps are a short (maximum three), practical, time-bound deterministic catalog. They complement—never replace—the physician's advice or reviewed automatic recommendations.
- No step recommends abrupt substance or alcohol withdrawal. No model call is used.

## Catalog behavior

- **Nutrition:** one flexible addition/substitution based on actual low-frequency answers (for example, add vegetables at lunch), without claiming nutritional deficiency.
- **Activity:** an entry-level 10-minute after-meal walk, five days in the week, only when reported aerobic minutes are below the existing target; a separate strength-session step when fewer than two sessions are reported.
- **Sleep:** a consistent wake time when sleep is a reported improvement area.
- **Connection/stress:** one small time-bound support or calming activity only when that pillar has an actual finding.

The panels are educational and not a diagnosis, treatment plan, or substitute for a clinician assessment.
